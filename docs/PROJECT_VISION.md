# Project Vision — Influence Partner App

**Last Updated:** 2026-06-02  
**Status:** Pre-development

---

## Problem Statement

Brands and marketers struggle to efficiently discover, evaluate, and manage influencer partnerships. Existing tools are either too enterprise-heavy, too manual, or lack integration between discovery and campaign management. Influencers on the other side lack visibility into brand partnerships that align with their niche and values.

---

## Vision

Build a two-sided platform that:

- **Helps brands** find the right influencers quickly, manage outreach, and track campaign ROI.
- **Helps influencers** surface partnership opportunities that match their audience and style.
- **Reduces friction** in the negotiation, contract, and content approval pipeline.

The long-term goal is a self-serve platform where a solo marketer can launch an influencer campaign in hours, not weeks.

---

## Target Audience

### Primary
- Small-to-mid-size brands (DTC, SaaS, consumer goods) running influencer programs with limited dedicated staff.

### Secondary
- Independent influencers and content creators (10k–500k followers) seeking brand deal pipelines.

---

## Core Value Propositions

1. **Unified workflow** — Discovery → Outreach → Agreement → Campaign → Analytics in one product.
2. **Relevance matching** — Smart filtering to surface high-fit partnerships, not just high reach.
3. **Speed** — Cut campaign setup time dramatically vs. manual processes.
4. **Transparency** — Both sides have clarity on deliverables, timelines, and status.

---

## Success Metrics (Long-Term)

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| Time from search to first contact | < 15 minutes |
| Campaign setup time | < 1 hour |
| Influencer response rate via platform | > 40% |
| Brand retention (monthly active) | > 60% at 90 days |

---

## Out of Scope (Long-Term Non-Goals)

- Becoming a social media management tool
- Direct content scheduling/publishing
- Replacing legal review for complex contracts
- Enterprise-tier seat licensing (Phase 1+)

---

## Guiding Principles

- **Simplicity over features** — Build less, build it well.
- **Data-informed** — Every feature justified by user behavior.
- **Both sides win** — Brand and influencer both get value; this isn't extractive.
- **Privacy by design** — Respect user data; don't sell it.
